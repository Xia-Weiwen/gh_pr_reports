# AI Analysis of Pull Requests for vllm-project/vllm during 20260614-20260621

### Key Takeaways
This week’s activity (583 PRs) is dominated by the industrialization of DeepSeek-V4 and Qwen-3.5 architectures, alongside aggressive kernel-level optimization for next-generation silicon, specifically NVIDIA Blackwell (SM100) and AMD MI355X (CDNA4). Top active areas include: 1. Hardware-specific kernel dispatch (ROCm/XPU/Blackwell), 2. DeepSeek V4/MLA architectural refinements, and 3. KV-Connector/Offloading scalability.
